# Bilingual standard

Beehive is read in English, te reo Māori, or both. The toggle is `English`, `Te reo Māori`, and `Bilingual` (`Reo rua`). The choice is the path (`/en`, `/mi`, `/bi`). The `beehive-reo` cookie only remembers the last choice so `/` can redirect. An old `?reo=` link is sent to the matching path.

## Names

Use Wellington City Council’s own bilingual names from Mātai Manapori, with macrons:

- Te Kaunihera o Pōneke | Council
- Te Taurapa | Council Planning and Finance Committee
- Te Hoe Matua | City Strategy and Delivery Committee
- Te Rauawa | Social, Cultural, and Environment Committee

The live catalogue spells Te Rauawa’s English name with a comma before “and”. Beehive keeps that form. The Council’s terms of reference PDF sometimes omits the comma. Both are cited rather than silently merged.

Pouiwi are Tākai Here partners, not ward councillors. Te Whanganui-a-Tara Māori Ward is the ward name from the Council. Beehive writes **Mātai Manapori** even though the site’s `og:site_name` is currently the unmacronned “Matai Manapori”.

Do not invent a te reo name for a body, role, or place. If it is not in a Council source, in Te Tiriti o Waitangi, or in a cited language authority, leave the English source and say the Māori summary has not been written.

## What gets translated

- Interface labels (navigation, the four receipt headings, vote rows) are written in both languages.
- A decision summary Beehive has authored is stored in both languages and stays tied to the clause it describes.
- Where no Māori summary has been authored, the page shows the published English clause or report summary and the sentence “Kāore anō kia tuhia he whakarāpopoto reo Māori.”
- Statutory clause text stays in the language the Council published.

## Macrons

Fonts are Newsreader and Public Sans with the Latin Extended subset, so ā, ē, ī, ō, and ū are not stripped. Search compares a folded copy of the text, but the URL slug keeps macrons. Official minutes remain the statutory record under the Local Government Act 2002, Schedule 7, clause 28.

## Te Tiriti o Waitangi

The Council’s terms of reference record obligations to iwi and Māori under Te Tiriti, including through the Local Government Act 2002. Tākai Here is the partnership with Taranaki Whānui ki Te Upoko o Te Ika, Te Rūnanganui o Te Āti Awa, and Te Rūnanga o Toa Rangatira. Pouiwi vote on the committees they are appointed to. At full Council they may attend and speak, and they do not vote. Beehive does not add a vote the terms of reference do not give them.
