2. General Business 
 
 
2.1 Non-Elected members - applying for exemptions from the provisions of 
the Local Government (System Improvements) Amendment Act 2026 
Moved Mayor Little, seconded Councillor Matthews the following motion  
Resolved 
That Te Kaunihera o Pōneke | Council:  
1. Receive the information. 
2. Approve that a joint application is prepared and lodged, together with Tiaki Wai 
shareholder councils which requests that the Minister of Local Government recommend 
that the Governor General grant an exception in relation to the Tiaki Wai Partners 
Committee, in relation to its current Mana Whenua Partner Representatives.   
3. Agree to delegate to the Chief Executive authority to work with the Chief Executives of 
other Shareholding Councils to prepare, finalise and lodge the joint application.   
4. Approve that an application is prepared and lodged which requests that the Minister of 
Local Government recommend that the Governor General grant an exception in relation 
to the governance body for the Disaster Resilience Fund, in relation to its expected 
Independent Member appointments.  
5. Approve that an application is prepared and lodged which requests that the Minister of 
Local Government recommend that the Governor General grant an exception in relation 
to the Council’s Committees of the Whole and subcommittees, in relation to its current 
Mana Whenua Partner Representatives.   
6. Approve that an application is prepared and lodged which requests that the Minister of 
Local Government recommend that the Governor General grant an exception in relation 
to Te Kura o Te Waka | Audit and Risk Committee, in relation to its current Independent 
Members (including the Chairperson).  
7. Agree to delegate to the Chairperson of Te Kaunihera o Pōneke | Council and the Chief 
Executive the authority to prepare, finalise and lodge each of the following applications: 
7.1. Application for exception for independent members of the governance body for the 
Disaster Resilience Fund; 
7.2. Application for exception for Pouiwi on Committees of the Whole and 
subcommittees; and 
7.3. Application for exception for Independent Members of Te Kura o te Waka | Audit and 
Risk Committee.

3. Committee Reports 
 
 
3.1 Report of the Te Taurapa | Council Planning and Finance Committee 
Meeting of 12 November 2026 
Moved Mayor Little, seconded Councillor Calvert 
Resolved 
Carried 
 
The Committee recommended that Te Kaunihera o Pōneke | Council:  
Southern Landfill Capital Works 
1. Receive the information. 
2. Agrees to continue using Stage 3 of the Southern Landfill within its existing footprint, to 
access up to approximately 170,000 tonnes of additional disposal airspace under the 
consent extended to 6 September 2029. This is instead of commencing use of SLEPO 
Cells 1 and 2 from 1 January 2028. 
3. Agrees to undertake capital works at Stage 3 of the Southern Landfill to create the 
additional airspace and reduce the risk of flooding and leachate release if the Carey's 
Gully tunnel becomes blocked. The works comprise a reinforced concrete-faced toe 
bund, erosion protection and drainage improvements, upgraded debris screening, and 
waterproofing. 
4. Approves unbudgeted funding of up to $5.5 million, funded through debt, for the design 
and construction of the Stage 3 extension and resilience works, noting that the release is 
subject to approval of the Project Brief and Business Case by the project SRO, in 
accordance with the Council's Investment Delivery Framework. 
5. Agrees the funding is phased as follows: 
5.1. FY26/27: $1.5 million ($130,000 opex for detailed design and the business case, 
and $1.37 million capex) 
5.1. FY27/28: $4.0 million 
 
Disaster Resilience Fund – Approval of Governance Documents  
2. Recommend that Te Kaunihera o Pōneke | Council:  
2.1 Approve the final Statement of Investment Policy and Objectives (SIPO). 
2.2 Approve the Disaster Resilience Fund Policy. 
2.3 Approve the Tahua Āwhina Aituā | Disaster Resilience Fund Committee Terms of 
Reference.
